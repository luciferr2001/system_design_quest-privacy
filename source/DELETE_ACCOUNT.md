# Delete your System Design Quest account

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

## Delete your account inside the app

You can delete your account and all associated data yourself, at any time:

1. Open System Design Quest and tap your profile picture at the top right.
2. Choose **Delete account**.
3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted immediately. If you signed in
   with Google, you may be asked to sign in again first, to confirm it is
   really you.

This works for both Google accounts and guest accounts.

## Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, or cannot open it:

1. Email **rathodrutik05@gmail.com** with the subject line "Delete my System
   Design Quest account".
2. Include the email address you signed in with (if you used Google
   Sign-In), or say that you used a guest account and roughly when you last
   used the app, so the developer can locate it. Guest accounts have no
   email address, so a guest account can only be found this way if you can
   describe it.
3. The developer will confirm your identity if needed and delete your
   account within **7 days**.

## What gets deleted

- Your Firebase Authentication account (Google Sign-In or guest). It is
  permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
- Your stored profile: display name, email address (if stored), photo URL,
  XP, level, streak, and onboarding answers (role, experience, goals, daily
  study time, age group).
- Your mission progress: which missions you completed, scores, and
  individual mission run details.

All of this is permanently removed from the app's database (Google Cloud
Firestore). Nothing is kept after deletion, other than what is described in
the next section.

## What is not affected

- Data that Google holds independently of this app (for example your Google
  Account itself, or advertising data already processed by Google AdMob) is
  governed by Google's own privacy policy and cannot be removed by this
  request. See https://policies.google.com/privacy.
- Routine backups and logs held by Google Cloud may take a short time to
  cycle out after deletion, in line with Google's own retention practices.
