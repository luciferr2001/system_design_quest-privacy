# Delete your System Design Quest account

_Last updated: 2026-09-28_

System Design Quest does not yet have an in-app "Delete account" button.
To delete your account and all associated data, follow the steps below.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **rathodrutik05@gmail.com** with the subject line
   "Delete my System Design Quest account".
2. Include the email address you signed in with (if you used Google
   Sign-In), or mention that you signed in as a guest and roughly when you
   last used the app (so the developer can locate the right account).
3. The developer will confirm your identity if needed, then delete your
   account within **7 days** of the request.

## What gets deleted

- Your Firebase Authentication account (Google Sign-In or guest/anonymous)
  is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.
- Your stored profile: display name, email address, photo URL, XP, level,
  streak, and onboarding answers (role, experience, goals, daily study
  time).
- Your mission progress: which missions you completed, scores, and
  individual mission run details (components placed, requirement results).

All of the above is permanently removed from System Design Quest's
database (Google Cloud Firestore). Nothing is retained after deletion
beyond the time needed to process the request.

## What is not affected

- Data Google itself holds independently of this app (e.g. your Google
  Account itself, or aggregate/anonymized advertising data already
  processed by Google AdMob) is governed by Google's own privacy policy
  and is not something this deletion request can remove - see
  https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Questions

For any questions about this process, contact rathodrutik05@gmail.com.
