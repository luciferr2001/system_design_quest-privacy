# Privacy Policy for System Design Quest

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

System Design Quest ("the App") is developed and run by **Rutik Rathod**, an
independent developer in India ("we", "the developer"). We are the controller
of the personal data described below. You can reach us at
**rathodrutik05@gmail.com** for anything in this policy, including privacy
questions, requests, and complaints.

## Summary

You can use the App with a Google account or as an anonymous guest. Your
learning progress is stored in the cloud so it can follow you across devices.
The App shows ads through Google AdMob. You can delete your account and data
yourself, inside the App, at any time.

## Who can use the App

The App is for people aged **13 and over**. Before you sign in, the App asks
which age group you belong to (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 or older).

- **Under 13:** the App is not offered to you. No account is created and no
  ads are shown.
- **13 to 17:** you can use the App. We treat your ad requests as coming from
  a teen, which means no personalized ads. If you are under 18, please use the
  App with the permission of a parent or guardian.
- **18 or older:** the normal experience.

We keep only your age group, not your date of birth. The App does not verify
your age; it relies on your answer.

## What data we collect

**Account data**

- If you sign in with **Google**: your display name and profile photo URL, as
  provided by Google. Google Sign-In also gives Firebase Authentication your
  email address so you can sign in; we do not copy your email into our own
  database and do not use it for anything else.
- If you sign in as a **guest**: an anonymous account identifier only. Guest
  accounts are tied to the app install that created them; if you reinstall
  or clear the App's data, that guest account cannot be recovered.
- A unique account identifier that links your progress to your account, and
  the dates your account was created and last used.
- Your **age group**, stored with your profile and on your device.

**Learning data**

- Your onboarding answers: your role, experience level, learning goals, and
  preferred daily study time.
- Your XP, level, streak, completed missions, and the details of missions you
  complete (components you placed, requirement results, score).

**Advertising data (collected by Google AdMob, not by us)**

- Your Android Advertising ID, device information (model, OS version,
  language, general region), IP address (used for ad delivery and to infer
  country or region), and how you interact with ads.

**Technical data.** Google Firebase and Google Play services, which the App
uses to run, process technical information such as IP address and device
details as part of providing their service.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR/UK GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Create your account, save and show your progress | Account data, learning data | Performance of the service you asked for |
| Keep younger users appropriately protected | Age group | Legitimate interests (protecting minors) |
| Show ads that fund the App | Advertising data | Your consent, where the law requires it (EEA, UK, Switzerland); otherwise legitimate interests, limited to non-personalized ads |
| Keep the service secure and prevent abuse | Technical data | Legitimate interests |

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we process your
personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign in after being
shown this notice.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use it for profiling or
automated decisions about you.

## Ads and your consent choices

The App shows banner and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Rewarded ads are
always optional.

- In the **EEA, UK, and Switzerland** (and where other laws require it),
  Google's consent form asks for your choices before ads are requested. You
  can change or withdraw your choices at any time from the account menu
  (your profile picture, then **Ad privacy options**, shown wherever consent
  applies).
- Where we have not collected consent, Google serves limited,
  non-personalized ads only.
- Users aged 13 to 17 receive no personalized ads.
- You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device
  settings (Settings, Google, Ads on most Android devices).

Google handles advertising data under its own policies:
https://policies.google.com/privacy and
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Who receives your data

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores and
  processes your account and learning data on our behalf.
- **Google Sign-In** is used only if you choose to sign in with Google.
- **Google AdMob** collects and uses advertising data as described above.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

## Where your data is stored, and transfers

Your account and learning data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore in
**Mumbai, India** (asia-south1). Google may process data in other countries.
Where personal data is transferred outside your country, Google relies on
safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses included in the Firebase
terms: https://firebase.google.com/terms/data-processing-terms

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning data until you delete your account. We do
not currently delete inactive accounts automatically; if we introduce that, we
will say so here. Your age group is also kept on your device until you clear
the App's data or uninstall it.

When you delete your account, your Firebase Authentication account, profile,
progress, and mission run data are permanently removed straight away. Google
may keep routine backups and logs for a short period under its own practices.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access your data, correct
it, delete it, receive a copy, object to or restrict certain processing, and
withdraw consent at any time. Under India's DPDP Act you can also nominate
another person to exercise these rights for you.

- **Delete your data:** inside the App, open the account menu (your profile
  picture) and choose **Delete account**. If you cannot open the App, see
  https://luciferr2001.github.io/system_design_quest-privacy/DELETE_ACCOUNT.html
- **Anything else** (access, correction, a copy of your data, or withdrawing
  consent): email rathodrutik05@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days and
  never later than the law allows.
- **Complaints:** contact us first and we will try to resolve it. You can
  also complain to your data protection authority, for example your national
  supervisory authority in the EEA, the ICO in the UK, or the Data Protection
  Board of India.

## Security

Data travels between the App and Google's servers over encrypted connections
(TLS). Database rules limit each signed-in user to their own data. We do not
store passwords; sign-in is handled by Google. No system is perfectly secure,
but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not show personalized ads to users aged 13 to 17, and we do not offer
  the App to users under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We will update the "Last updated" date above whenever this policy changes, and
will tell you in the App if a change is significant.
